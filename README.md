### ALGO-000014: Graph isomorphism complexity
- **Subfield:** concrete algorithms
- **Open:** Is graph isomorphism in P?
- **Status:** Open. Best: quasipolynomial time n^{polylog n} (Babai 2016, with Helfgott's simplification). Not believed NP-complete (would collapse PH by Boppana–Håstad–Zachos). Practical solvers (nauty, Traces) handle huge instances.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** GI is the canonical "NP-intermediate" candidate; its orbit-closure structure connects to group theory and practical isomorphism testing (chemistry, circuits).
- **Key works:** L. Babai, "Graph isomorphism in quasipolynomial time", STOC 2016 (arXiv:1512.03547); J. Köbler, U. Schöning, J. Torán, "The Graph Isomorphism Problem: Its Structural Complexity", Birkhäuser 1993
